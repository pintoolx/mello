# Mello design context

- Audience: Taiwanese finance, risk, and engineering teams evaluating an agent purchase-control MVP on a projector.
- Brand: enterprise control room, not a retail crypto product. Figma palette uses #42F658 primary, #8CF699 secondary, #76DAA1 tertiary, #111111 ink, and #FAFAFA paper.
- Typography: Poppins for Latin display and headings; Noto Sans TC fallback for Chinese operational copy.
- Signature: an evidence rail connects intent, policy, payment, service, invoice, and reconciliation.
- UI rules: large type, sharp panels, restrained radii, no gradients, no glassmorphism, no ornamental crypto motifs.
- Copy: concrete, audit-oriented, concise. Always label simulated settlement and test invoices clearly.
