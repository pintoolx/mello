# Mello design context

- Audience: Taiwanese finance, risk, procurement, and engineering teams evaluating an agent purchase-control product in a business setting.
- Use case: the public website explains Mello as a real product without staging the seeded scenario. The separate console provides an operable product preview that Canfly can record manually as demo material.
- Brand personality: documentary, procedural, trustworthy. Reference enterprise forms, internal-control software, stamped records, and legacy ERP information density without becoming unusable.
- Brand: enterprise operations system, not a retail crypto product or neon Web3 dashboard. Figma green remains a restrained status accent alongside navy ink, warm paper, gray table headers, and hairline rules.
- Typography: restrained Noto Sans TC for operational copy, a document-like serif for marketing headings, and Roboto Mono only for IDs, times, and machine records.
- Signature: purchase intent becomes a numbered document whose policy, payment, service, invoice, and reconciliation records can be audited in one view.
- UI rules: square enterprise panels, compact controls, real tables, document metadata, restrained type sizes, no gradients, no glassmorphism, no ornamental crypto motifs, no oversized demo-state typography.
- Copy: concrete, audit-oriented, concise. Always label simulated settlement and test invoices clearly.
